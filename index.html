Bluetamp Academy & Roastery

Learn to pull a proper shot.

Toon — bronze medalist at the Thailand National Barista Championship 2020 and silver at Leva Speed 2023 — built the Chokchai 4 Roastery to train the next generation of Bangkok baristas. Short courses for hobbyists, intensive paths for working pros, and consulting for cafes opening their first bar.

01

Barista Foundation

Single-day course — espresso fundamentals, milk texture, latte art basics. Open to total beginners.

02

Professional Path

Multi-week intensive for working baristas — extraction theory, sensory, bar workflow, customer flow.

03

Cafe Consulting

Opening a cafe? Toon consults on menu design, bar layout, training plans, equipment specs.

04

Roastery Tours

See the green, smell the chaff. Bluetamp Roastery roasts every bag sold at the cafe and on Shopee.
